## Where this leaves the client-usable score

The path is unchanged: landmark retrain gives P(blocked) risk bands for pending projects; the negative audit unlocks P(opposition emerges); the cost layer translates; the two-mode map delivers. The map delivery is now built and waiting on the models rather than the other way around. What changed in 2026-07 is the understanding of the bottleneck: it is not modeling and it is not UI, it is Phase 1/2 data quality. The phases went recursive, and that is by design of the defensibility principle, not a stall.
