What this page is. The platform measures its own coverage against an external census, turns the gaps into ranked work, builds and promotes candidates through a gate, promotes or blocks harvested signals with a recorded reason, and retrains models that promote only on holding calibration. Every one of those steps writes an audit trail. This page reads those trails. Nothing here is an estimate or a projection; where an input is missing, the panel is omitted rather than filled in.
Every number on this page is read live from data/operations_summary.json, which operations_summary.py writes from the pipeline's own audit trails on each run. The summary is computed in a module with self-tests rather than in this page, so a figure a reader sees here is derived once and checked, and the two-megabyte signal promotion trail never has to cross a browser.
