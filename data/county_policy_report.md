**Internal diagnostic. Associations are predictive, not causal. Scores are out-of-fold cross-validated probabilities of the county having an enacted restrictive policy given its profile; they are not probabilities that a new project draws opposition.**

## Outcome and frame

- Frame: 3144 counties. Puerto Rico municipios (n = 78) are excluded from the model and retained in the aggregate layer: no presidential margin exists and land-use institutions differ.
- Positive: county has at least one enacted restrictive policy, n = 329 (base rate 10.5 pct).
- The outcome is an enacted public record, which reduces but does not remove detection-bias exposure relative to opposition-presence outcomes.

## Validation

- Repeated stratified CV (5 folds x 5 repeats, seed 7).
- Specification selected by a registered rule, not a fixed variable list: 'tier1_plus_size' with C = 1.0, 5 variables. Tier 3 variables are strongly collinear (density x population +0.87, income x education +0.69), so a coefficient's sign can flip across folds; only specifications where every coefficient holds its sign across all 25 folds are admissible, and among those the rule takes the highest median AUC, then the most variables retained. Candidates are regenerated from the data on every run, so a variable enters when it holds a direction and leaves when it stops; see data/county_model_spec_history.csv for every such change and its evidence. Requiring stability cost 0.0018 median AUC against the best specification tried. Under shrinkage, coefficient magnitudes are compressed toward zero by design; read relative ordering and sign, not absolute size.
- ROC AUC: 0.80 (p10 0.75, p90 0.83).
- Brier: 0.080 vs base-rate Brier 0.094.
- Raw-score calibration slope 0.99 (shrinkage compresses raw scores); the model therefore includes a cross-fitted recalibration layer. Post-recalibration slope 0.99, intercept -0.01. Use calibrated_score from the scores file.

## Trust tiers

- Tier 1: political margin, existing DC count. Low detection-bias exposure; safest for external use.
- Tier 3: population, density, income, education. These variables also predict news coverage; on this public-record outcome the exposure is reduced, and it is still reported.

## Standing caveats

- Scores are out-of-fold: no county is scored by a model that saw it in training.
- The score is a resemblance measure over county profiles, calibrated to the observed enactment base rate. It is not a forecast of opposition to any specific project.
- Inputs are QC-gated upstream by county_aggregator.py; the input manifest is embedded in county_policy_metrics.json for reproducibility.
