## Standing caveats

- A census can raise a positive grade and can never on its own clear a county. Its silence about a county is not a finding.
- Grade A requires two distinct independence classes. Two compilations that both read press coverage are one class, not two.
- `in_force_as_of` is populated only where a source reports an active or extended instrument. It is blank for historical instruments, and blank is not a statement that one lapsed.
- This ledger is additive. It does not change has_enacted_restrictive, and no model reads a grade as a feature.
