A bill at `partial` scope carries data center provisions inside a vehicle that is mostly about something else. Its roll calls are published and are never given a direction: a vote on the vehicle is not a position on the provision, and the roll call cannot distinguish them. The module enforces this — an override may assert a direction only on a bill it also declares `primary`.

## Standing limits

- A stance is never netted into a single pro-or-anti score. Supporting a reporting requirement is not opposing data centers, and opposing a tax exemption is not either.
- A governing body action is attributed to the body, never to an individual member. This layer does not know how a board split.
- A `single_act` record is one vote, not a pattern.
- A bill admitted by human override at `partial` scope publishes its votes with no direction, because the vote was cast on the whole vehicle rather than on its data center provisions.
- Roll calls exist only for states where OpenStates publishes them and only for bills this repository has already matched, so absence of a record is not evidence of an absent position.
- A `recorded_vote_no_direction` row states how someone voted and makes no claim about what the vote meant.
